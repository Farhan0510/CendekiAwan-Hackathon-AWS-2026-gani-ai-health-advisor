# GANI AI: Smart Health Decision Assistant

GANI AI is a digital health decision assistant built using AWS PartyRock.

It helps users decide what to do when they feel unwell by providing:
- Urgency level: Green, Yellow, or Red
- 24-hour action plan
- Red flag warning signs
- Doctor communication helper
- Healthcare access guidance
- Family/caregiver share message

## Problem

Many people are unsure whether symptoms require self-care, a doctor visit, or emergency care. This can cause panic, delayed treatment, or unnecessary hospital visits.

## Solution

GANI AI focuses on the question:

**“What should I do next?”**

The app does not diagnose or prescribe medicine. It provides safe, structured guidance to support better decision-making.

## Key Features

### Smart Triage
Classifies urgency into:
- Green: self-care and monitoring
- Yellow: see a doctor soon
- Red: seek emergency help

### 24-Hour Action Plan
Gives practical steps on what to do now, what to monitor, and when to seek care.

### Doctor Communication Helper
Creates a short note users can show or read to a doctor.

### Share With Family
Generates a simple message users can send to family or caregivers.

### Access Guidance
Adapts guidance based on whether the user has nearby clinic access, no clinic nearby, or emergency access only.

## Safety

GANI AI:
- Does not diagnose
- Does not prescribe medication
- Does not replace professional medical advice
- Directs emergency cases to emergency services
